Voice cues
No special outfit needed — quick tips
  • Tuck in long tops, or tie them at the waist, so the hip line shows.
  • A dupatta, belt or string tied at the waist marks the hips clearly.
  • Roll trousers above the ankle. Bare feet or thin socks help the heels show.
  • Avoid clothes the same colour as the wall behind.
  • Light in front of the client, not behind them.
